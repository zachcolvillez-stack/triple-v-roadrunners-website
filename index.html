About Us

Your local Malaga smash repair workshop.

Triple V Roadrunners Smash Repairs is a licensed Malaga smash repair and panel paint workshop (motor vehicle repairer MRB11326) focused on quality repairs, clean finishes and straightforward service. Whether your vehicle has accident damage, dents, scratches or paintwork that needs attention, we help get it looking sharp again.

We work on private vehicles and local business fleets from our Vale Street workshop, handling everything from a single scratched panel through to full accident damage repairs — panel straightening, parts replacement where repair is not the right answer, surface preparation and colour-matched refinishing.
