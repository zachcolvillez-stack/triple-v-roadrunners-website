Who we are

Triple V Roadrunners Smash Repairs is a smash repair and panel paint workshop operating from Unit 3/19 Vale Street, Malaga WA 6090, in the Malaga industrial area. We are a licensed motor vehicle repairer, licence number MRB11326.

We work on private vehicles and local business fleets, handling the full range of body repair work in-house — accident damage, panel straightening, parts replacement where repair is not the right answer, surface preparation and colour-matched refinishing. That matters because a repair that gets handed between a panel shop and a separate paint shop is a repair where nobody owns the final result.

How we work

  • Inspect first, quote in writing. No phone estimates that change once the car arrives.
  • Repair versus replace, explained. You get told which parts of the quote are repairs and which are replacements, and the reasoning.
  • No work you have not approved. If dismantling reveals hidden damage — and in collision work it often does — you get a call and a revised quote first.
  • Metalwork before filler. Filler is for final levelling, not for making up shape a panel should have been returned to.
  • Colour matched off the vehicle, not off the paint code, because Perth sun moves every car away from its original colour.
  • Blended into the adjacent panels where the finish needs it, so the repair does not stop dead at a panel line.
  • Honest advice about what can wait. If damage is cosmetic and not going to cause you a problem, we will say so.

What we work on

Passenger cars, utes, vans and light commercial vehicles. Damage from a single scuffed panel through to multi-panel collision repairs. The services we offer are:

  • Smash repairs — accident damage of all kinds
  • Panel beating — panel straightening and body line restoration
  • Accident repairs — full collision repair process
  • Car paint repairs — refinishing and colour matching
  • Bumper repairs — repair as an alternative to replacement
  • Dent and scratch repairs — minor body work
  • Car resprays — panel, section and full resprays
