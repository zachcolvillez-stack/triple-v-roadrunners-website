What to send for a quote

We need to see the vehicle to give you a firm quote, but photos let us give you a useful initial idea over the phone or by email. The most helpful set:

  • One wide shot of the whole vehicle from the damaged side.
  • One shot of the damaged panel in full, including the panels either side of it — we need to see where the damage stops.
  • Close shots of the damage itself from two or three angles.
  • A shot of anything you can see behind the damage if a panel is pushed in or a bumper is hanging.
  • Your vehicle's make, model and year.
  • Whether the vehicle is drivable, and whether anything is rubbing, leaking or not latching.

Safety first: if fluid is leaking, a wheel is not sitting straight, a bonnet will not latch, or something is rubbing a tyre, do not drive it. Call us and describe it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a repair quote?

Call 0425 559 576, email vincepanelandpaint@gmail.com with photos, or use the quote form on the homepage. For a firm quote we need to see the vehicle, but photos let us give you a useful initial idea first.

Are quotes free?

Yes. Quotes are free, given in writing after we inspect the vehicle, and itemised so you can see what the panel work, parts, preparation and refinishing each involve.

What information do you need from me?

Your vehicle's make, model and year, clear photos of the damage including a wide shot showing where the damage stops, and whether the vehicle is drivable or anything is rubbing, leaking or not latching.
